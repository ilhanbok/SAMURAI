Utilities

The five Utilities.py operations. Files are processed in a throw-away server workspace and the result is offered as a download.

MERGE

Voxel-wise logical OR of every uploaded mask — they must share a shape.

ZERO

Blank a slice range of spurious segmentation. Axis numbering follows Utilities.py: 0 blanks along the third array dimension, 1 the second, 2 the first.

DUPLICATE

Copy one slice onto another (1-based indices, third array dimension).

VOLUME

Volume of each uploaded mask, in mm³, plus mean and standard deviation.

IRV

Inter-rater variability between two sets of masks, paired by sort order. Cohen IRV < 0.5 is poor, 0.5–0.7 good, > 0.7 excellent; the Dice score is reported alongside.
